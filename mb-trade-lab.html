Accounts

Connect a live MT4/MT5 account (including most prop firms, which run on the same platforms) and its closed trades get logged here automatically — no manual entry. Currently supports MT4/MT5 via a small Expert Advisor you install once in your own terminal; nothing is installed on our side and no credentials are ever shared with us.

Connect an Account

Finish Setup in MT4/MT5

Three steps and closed trades start flowing in automatically.

MB_TradeLab_EA.mq5

In MT5: Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, enable "Allow WebRequest for listed URL," and add your Supabase project's domain. Open the downloaded file in MetaEditor and compile (F7), then drag it onto any chart and paste the Webhook URL above into its Inputs tab. Your next closed trade should appear here within a few seconds — check the "Experts" log tab in MT5 if it doesn't.

Where do I install this? An Expert Advisor only runs on MT5 desktop — a Windows PC, a Mac (via your broker's native MT5 app), or a VPS if you don't have either running all the time. The MT5 mobile app cannot run an EA at all — there's nothing to install there, and this setup can't be done from a phone.

Trading from your phone still works. Once this is running on your desktop or VPS, you can keep taking trades from the MT5 mobile app as normal — they sync here automatically too, since the EA watches the whole account's history, not just trades it places itself. Just make sure that one desktop/VPS instance stays running.

If MT5 was closed when you traded: just reopen it with the EA still attached — on startup it automatically scans back and syncs anything that happened while it was off, so nothing needs re-entering manually.
