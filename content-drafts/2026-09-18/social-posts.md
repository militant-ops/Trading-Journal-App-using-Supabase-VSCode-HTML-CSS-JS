## Post 3 — General trading/market education tip (X/Twitter + LinkedIn)

**A tip that costs nothing and most traders skip: log the setups you didn't take, not just the ones you did.**

A missed A+ setup is data. Skipping it from your review means you only ever see half your edge — the trades you took, never the discipline (or hesitation) that shaped which ones you didn't.

Track both. Review both. That's the full picture.
